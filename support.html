Cornerman Support

Questions, problems or feedback: email support.cornerman@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Profile → About), and what happened.

Subscription

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Cornerman, or in the app go to Profile → Subscription → Manage Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Will I be charged after the free trial?

Yes, unless you cancel before the trial ends. The app tells you when your trial ends, and sends a reminder two days before if notifications are on. Apple's payment sheet shows the exact terms before you confirm.

I paid, but the app is asking me to subscribe

Tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen, or in Profile → Subscription. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you paid with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the Cornerman purchase.

Your data

Where is my training data kept?

On your iPhone. Your fighter, stats, plan and training history are saved on the device and included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups.

I got a new iPhone. How do I keep my fighter?

Set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one, and your fighter comes with it. Then tap Restore Purchases if the app asks you to subscribe. Deleting the app deletes its data, so don't delete it before you've backed up.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Profile → Delete Account & All Data. It erases everything Cornerman keeps on your phone and can't be undone. Deleting your data doesn't cancel your subscription; cancel it as described above.

Training

My plan includes something I can't do

Update your gear or injuries in Profile → Edit Profile. Choose Apply now to rebuild this week's plan straight away (a new injury always does). The plan only includes sessions that suit what you've entered.

I'm sick or injured

Tap I'm sick or hurt on the Dashboard to pause for up to 7 days. Your streak is safe and your stats don't slip for those days.

Cornerman isn't medical advice. If you're injured or something hurts, get it checked by a doctor or physio before training on it.

I'm not getting reminders

Check Profile → Notifications in the app, and that notifications for Cornerman are allowed in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications.
