Cornerman Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026
Who we are: Cornerman ("we", "us") makes the Cornerman app. We are the controller of the personal information described here.
Contact: support.cornerman@gmail.com

This policy explains what Cornerman collects, where it goes, who can see it, and how to delete it. We've tried to write it plainly. If anything is unclear, email us.

The short version

  • Most of what you tell Cornerman stays on your phone: your profile, injuries, equipment, workouts and photos.
  • If you sign in with Apple to use Fight Camps, some of it goes to our server so your campmates can see your progress. Your injuries, age, equipment and workout details are never sent.
  • Campmates in your camps see your display name, weight class, style, stats, streak, fight record and whether you've trained today. They also see fist bumps you send them, your camp's total training days for the week and its goal, and who took last week's crown. If you miss a planned training day, they're told that you missed it, and nothing more.
  • Subscriptions go through Apple, and RevenueCat keeps track of them.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you. Once you've told the app you're 13 or older, we count anonymous app usage, like how many people finish each setup step, with Aptabase. It never includes your name, age, injuries, training or any account or device ID, and you can turn it off in Profile → Privacy → Share anonymous usage.
  • If the app hits an error, a crash report goes to Sentry so we can fix it. It says what went wrong and where in the app, never who you are or what you logged, and it's never sent for anyone under 13.
  • What's on your phone is included in your own iPhone backups, so a restore or a new phone brings it back.
  • You can delete everything from Profile → Delete Account & All Data.

1. Information that stays on your device

When you set up Cornerman and use it, the app stores the following on your phone only:

  • Profile: fighter name, age, weight class, fighting style, goal, training days per week, session length, self-ratings, experience level and training focus.
  • Health-related details you choose to enter: injuries (for example "knee" or "shoulder"), used only to keep unsafe sessions out of your plan.
  • Equipment you have access to.
  • Training: your plan, logged workouts, stats, XP, streaks, badges, challenges, Comeback progress and days you paused training because you were sick or hurt.
  • Fight Card photo: if you add one, it stays on your device. The app asks for photo access only when you choose a photo, and asks separately before saving a Fight Card to your photo library.
  • Settings: notification preferences.

This information is not sent to us, and we can't see it. It stays until you delete the app or use Delete Account & All Data.

Your device backups. This information is included in your own iPhone backups, in iCloud or on a computer, so restoring a backup or moving to a new iPhone brings your fighter back. Those backups belong to you and are managed by Apple and your settings; we can't see them. The app also keeps one earlier copy of your saved data on the phone, so it can recover if the latest save is ever damaged.

Daily motivation reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. They don't involve our server.

Sharing to Instagram happens only when you tap Share. The Fight Card image goes straight to the Instagram app on your phone, and we don't see it.

2. Information we collect when you use Fight Camps

Fight Camps (the social features) require signing in with Apple, and you must be 13 or older to use them (see section 10). Fight Camps are currently available only in test versions of the app. This section applies when you use them.

When you sign in with Apple

Apple gives us:

  • A unique identifier for your Apple ID in Cornerman.
  • Your email address, or an Apple private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email".
  • Your name, only the first time you sign in and only if you choose to share it. We may use it as your starting display name.

We store these with our backend provider, Supabase (see section 4), to run your account.

3. What other users can see

Campmates (people in a camp with you) can see:

  • your display name, weight class and fighting style
  • your stats, current streak and fight record
  • your last training date
  • whether you've trained today. Only that you trained: never what, how long or how hard
  • fist bumps you send them. When you fist bump a campmate's session, they see your name in a notification
  • your camp's weekly goal: the camp's total training days for the week and the goal. Never how many days each person trained
  • last week's crown: who trained the most days in the camp that week
  • that you've missed a planned training day, through a tap-out alert the next morning. They learn nothing else about your plan or training. You get a private reminder the evening before.

Campmates cannot see your email, age, injuries, equipment, workout details, how many days you've trained, time zone, paused days, or who you've blocked or reported.

People who aren't in a camp with you can't see anything about you.

Blocking: if you block someone, or they block you, neither of you can see the other in any camp, and you won't get alerts about each other. They aren't told.

5. How we use information

  • To provide the app: build your plan, track your progress, and run camps and alerts.
  • To manage your subscription.
  • To keep the community safe: enforce display name rules, handle reports and blocks, and stop abuse.
  • To respond when you contact us.
  • To understand, in anonymous totals, where people stop during setup, so we can make it better.
  • To find and fix errors, from crash reports.

We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising, and we don't make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Reports and moderation

You can report a campmate from the camp roster. A report includes the reason you pick, any note you add, your user ID, their user ID and display name, and the camp. The person you report isn't told who reported them.

Reports are emailed to our support inbox and reviewed within 24 hours. We may remove content, remove someone from a camp, or delete their account (see the Terms of Use).

Display names are checked automatically for offensive words and for names that impersonate Cornerman staff.

7. How long we keep information

  • On your device: until you delete the app or use Delete Account & All Data. Copies in your own iCloud or computer backups stay until those backups are replaced or you delete them.
  • On our server: your profile, camp memberships, push token, alert settings and blocks stay until you change them or delete your account. Other records are deleted automatically once they're no longer needed (table below), and deleting your account removes all of them straight away.
  • Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days, and then deleted.
  • Anonymous usage counts are kept by Aptabase for up to 5 years, and then deleted. They contain nothing that identifies you.
  • Reports are kept for up to 365 days for moderation, even if either account is deleted. If an account is deleted, its user ID is removed from the report, but the copy of the display name and the note stay.
  • Failed Sign in with Apple disconnections: if we couldn't disconnect Sign in with Apple when you deleted your account, we keep your user ID, Apple identifier, email and the error for up to 90 days so we can follow up.
  • Backups: our database provider's current plan doesn't include scheduled backups, so deleted data isn't kept in any backup we can restore. If we turn backups on, they'll be kept for no more than 7 days and then deleted.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as we need them to help you, and then deleted.
  • RevenueCat and Apple keep purchase records under their own policies.

8. Deleting your account

Go to Profile → Delete Account & All Data. If you're signed in, you'll confirm with Apple once more. We then:

  1. Disconnect Cornerman from your Apple ID (revoke Sign in with Apple).
  2. Delete your account and everything listed in section 2 from our server.
  3. Erase the data stored in the app on your phone: your fighter and its earlier copy, scheduled reminders and the Fight Card photo the app saved.

If disconnecting from Apple fails twice, we still delete your account and data, and follow up on the Apple side ourselves. You can also remove Cornerman in Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple.

Copies of your fighter in your own iCloud or computer backups aren't touched; delete those backups in iOS Settings if you want them gone.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

9. Notifications

Cornerman asks before sending notifications. You can turn campmate alerts (missed training days, the Monday recap and fist bumps) off for a single camp from the camp screen, turn them all off in Profile → Notifications → Campmate alerts, turn off just fist bumps in Profile → Notifications → Fist bumps, or turn off all notifications in iOS Settings.

10. Children

Cornerman's solo training features are open to all ages, and the information they use stays on your device (section 1). We don't receive it.

Nothing is sent to Aptabase until an age of 13 or over has been entered. The welcome and age screens are never counted, the Share anonymous usage switch stays off under 13, nothing is sent once an age under 13 is entered, and nothing is ever sent for a profile under 13 or with no age on file. Crash reports (section 4) are never sent for a profile under 13 or with no age on file.

Accounts and Fight Camps require you to be 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. In the app:

  • Fight Camps aren't shown to anyone whose profile age is under 13.
  • Before signing in with Apple, you must confirm you're 13 or older.

If we learn that we've collected personal information from a child under 13, we'll delete it and the account. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email support.cornerman@gmail.com.

11. Security

Data sent to our server is encrypted in transit. Private information such as training dates, push tokens, blocks and reports is locked so that only our server can read it, never other users. Server keys never ship in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Because most of your information never leaves your phone, we usually hold very little about you, and we may need to ask you to confirm your Fight Camps account before acting on a request.
